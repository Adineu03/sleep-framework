\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Theoretical contribution:}
The paper's central theoretical result is a causal decomposition of why biologically-faithful consolidation failed---and the demonstration that each cause, once named, is removable. The recall floor that survived every within-paradigm intervention (perfect replay data, safety sweeps, a trained retrieval skill) decomposed into three stacked errors: substrate (the adapter sat in attention projections chosen by anatomical analogy, not the mid-stack MLPs where causal tracing places factual associations), surface statistics (single-wording training produces string memorization, visible as cloze success with recall failure---and paraphrase diversity moves the two metrics in \emph{opposite} directions), and calibration (a hard clip tight enough to erase the write after training performed it). This carries a general lesson for biologically-inspired machine learning: biological \emph{mechanisms} can transfer while biological \emph{anatomy} does not. Tagging, resource allocation, replay, and sleep-phase consolidation all survived the transfer; the hippocampus-to-neocortex layer mapping was the part that failed, and the transformer's own ``anatomy''---as revealed by the editing literature \citep{geva2021transformer,meng2022rome,meng2023memit}---had to replace it. The diagnostic findings stand independently: the recognition--recall dissociation for attention-injected memory (now reproduced under an experimental manipulation via the warm-up), the near-total failure of proxy self-validation ($93$--$100\%$ false confirmation on every model), and the horizon-and-seed instability of unconstrained continual-learning baselines, which the validation matrix sharpens into an asymmetric claim: constrained arms can be made seed-stable; unconstrained arms are the lottery.

\paragraph{The EWC dissociation:}
The matched-arm showdown isolates a distinction the continual-learning literature tends to blur: \emph{preservation} and \emph{acquisition} are different capabilities. EWC-only achieves the best background-damage numbers in the matrix while its recall never rises above naive fine-tuning's---the Fisher penalty determines what the model \emph{keeps}, not what it can \emph{extract}. The repaired pipeline's advantage comes from the write path (where and on what surface statistics the gradient lands), not from protecting the result harder. Systems that report only forgetting metrics would score EWC-only the winner of an experiment it loses three-fold on recall.

\paragraph{Practical implications:}
For practitioners, the operational lessons now come in two tiers. From the diagnosis: direct-write KV memory supports recognition-style tasks on a frozen backbone but not free-form generation, and a cheap retrieval-aware warm-up does not change that; proxy self-validation must be audited against an external criterion before it is trusted; and continual-learning comparisons involving unconstrained arms need horizon sweeps and seed spreads, because a single run's ordering is lottery-decided. From the repair: consolidation adapters for factual content belong in mid-stack MLPs, not attention; train on paraphrase diversity including question--answer forms, or the system will memorize strings it cannot use; distillation from the model's own in-context behaviour is both the strongest and the gentlest write we measured; and safety machinery should be calibrated by ablation in its design regime---ours was simultaneously too tight (erasing writes single-cycle) and, once moderated, genuinely load-bearing (preventing a $24\times$ damage blow-up at 1.5B that unconstrained fine-tuning incurs).

\paragraph{Challenges:}
Several scope constraints bound the strength of our claims:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2.4em,itemsep=0.15em,topsep=0.2em]
    \item[(i)] We test four models across three families, all dense decoder-only architectures at $\leq$8B; mixture-of-experts and much larger scales remain untested.
    \item[(ii)] We test \emph{synthetic} facts in 10 templates with template-generated paraphrases; natural document content, and naturally-occurring paraphrase distributions, may differ.
    \item[(iii)] Llama-3.1-8B is validated for recall gains but not for preservation: no tested clip reaches our damage bar, its multi-cycle BCP compounds ($4.3$--$9.2$), and EWC-only is competitive with the repaired pipeline on that family. We flag it as \emph{not yet tuned} and treat per-family learning-rate optimisation as required, not optional.
    \item[(iv)] We force \emph{eager attention}; SDPA and FlashAttention paths handle 4D additive masks differently and we did not characterize them after the gating mechanism was finalized.
    \item[(v)] No configuration holds $\mathrm{BCP}<1.05$ over ten cycles---damage leaks $\approx +0.12$/cycle in every arm---and cumulative recall decays as early batches fade, because the engine rehearses only the current batch. The validated system is the best of its cohort, not a solved problem.
    \item[(vi)] The multi-cycle validation uses two seeds per arm (with five seeds single-cycle); the diagnosis-era lottery finding cautions against over-reading two-seed orderings, though every multi-cycle claim we make held on both seeds and the constrained arm's variance is demonstrably small.
\end{itemize}

Where variance was measured it now separates into three regimes. The diagnostic floors are tight (DRA SD $\leq 0.003$). The repaired pipeline is tight (single-cycle SD $\leq 0.05$ on every model; multi-cycle across-seed spread $\approx 0.005$). The unconstrained baseline is not (multi-cycle damage varying up to $24\times$ seed-to-seed at 1.5B)---and we report that asymmetry as a finding. The strongest claim our evidence supports is: on these models, on this dataset, under pre-registered gates, the repaired pipeline's advantages replicate on every seed we ran.

\paragraph{Ethical considerations:}
Continual-learning systems for language models, if they work, would reduce retraining costs substantially and enable models to learn from interaction. The repair reported here moves that prospect closer, which sharpens rather than softens the concerns: persistent memory of user inputs, model memorization of sensitive information, and asymmetric power between systems that can update themselves and users who cannot. Our architecture stores user inputs (tagged spans) in a KV bank and consolidates a subset into permanent weights; deployed naively, this would amount to silent on-device data retention---now with a consolidation path that demonstrably works. We argue that any deployed continual-learning system must include explicit user controls over what is encoded, what is consolidated, and what is permanent, alongside mechanisms for selective forgetting on user request. The self-grading result adds a safety dimension: a system that certifies its own learning against an internal proxy may report successful consolidation of information it cannot reproduce---or fail to report retention of information it can. External validation gates of the kind we adopt should be considered a safety component, not merely a metrological one.

\section{Conclusion and Future Scope}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We implemented a biologically-faithful memory consolidation architecture for transformer language models, diagnosed its failure to the point of causal decomposition, and repaired it. The diagnosis stands on four models from three families, five seeds each: direct-write KV memory produces recognition without recall (a floor of $\mathrm{DRA}\approx 0.006$ from models that follow the same facts in context at up to $0.99$); a retrieval-aware warm-up moves recognition but never recall; internal surprise-based validation false-confirms $93$--$100\%$ of consolidations; and unconstrained continual-learning baselines are horizon- and seed-unstable. The repair stands on the same models under pre-registered gates: relocating consolidation to mid-stack MLPs, training on paraphrase diversity, distilling from an in-context teacher, and moderating the safety clip raises single-cycle recall to $17$--$125\times$ the floor ($0.750 \pm 0.033$ on Mistral-7B), beats naive fine-tuning on cumulative ten-cycle recall on all four models on both seeds, beats a matched EWC-only baseline three-fold on the anchor model, and prevents the seed-random damage catastrophe that destroys unconstrained fine-tuning at small scale. The central lesson generalises beyond this system: in transferring biological memory to transformers, the \emph{mechanisms} (tagging, selective replay, staged consolidation, sleep-phase scheduling) survive; the \emph{anatomy} (which layers play hippocampus and neocortex) must come from the transformer's own causal structure, and the knowledge-editing literature already maps it.

\paragraph{Future scope:}
Our results give follow-up work specific targets rather than vague directions:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2.4em,itemsep=0.15em,topsep=0.2em]
    \item[(i)] \emph{Interleaved rehearsal}: cumulative recall decays because the engine rehearses only the current batch; biological sleep interleaves old consolidations with new. Adding rehearsal of prior cycles' facts (or their paraphrases) to the replay mix is the clearest next mechanism and directly targets the early-batch fade.
    \item[(ii)] \emph{Per-family optimisation}: Llama-3.1-8B gains recall like every other model but bleeds damage that no clip setting contains; its learning-rate and signal-calibration surface is unexplored. The matrix provides the harness; the sweep is cheap.
    \item[(iii)] \emph{Closing the damage leak}: every arm leaks $\approx +0.12$ BCP per cycle. Candidate mechanisms: per-cycle Fisher refresh against the \emph{original} base model rather than the drifting anchor, and consolidation-aware early stopping per fact.
    \item[(iv)] \emph{The recognition--recall gap at training scale}: our warm-up failed at $\leq$300 steps; the informative next experiment remains the same intervention at Memorizing-Transformers scale with per-family learning rates, now with the mid-MLP consolidation path available as the comparison arm.
    \item[(v)] \emph{Beyond dense $\leq$8B decoders}: extend the matrix to mixture-of-experts and larger scales, where the editing literature's localisation maps---and therefore the substrate correction---may differ.
\end{itemize}

We release the implementation, formalization, pre-registrations, full experimental logs, and all validation-phase runs to enable that work.
