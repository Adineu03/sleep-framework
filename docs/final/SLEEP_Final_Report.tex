% ===== Preamble ============================================================
Professor Guha,

The report of 8 August closed the programme as a diagnosis: a universal recognition--recall
dissociation, a universal proxy-validation failure, and a replicated null on the warm-up repair.
Your feedback on that version proposed a different reading --- that the consolidation failure was a
\emph{localisation} error, not a stability--plasticity limit --- and three concrete changes: move the
consolidation adapter from top-third attention to mid-stack MLP (per the causal-tracing literature),
train on paraphrase diversity rather than a single wording, and distil from an in-context teacher.
You also asked that adaptation and injection be decoupled, and that knowledge-presence evaluation use
greedy decoding.

We implemented all of it, isolated each factor, and then validated the resulting pipeline across the
full four-model matrix under pre-registered gates. The one-line summary: \textbf{your localisation
hypothesis was correct, it was the dominant cause, and the repaired system now beats both naive
fine-tuning and an EWC baseline in its design regime.} The recall floor of $0.006$ that survived
every earlier repair attempt rises to $0.750$ on Mistral-7B (five seeds) under the new recipe. This
report gives the numbers in the order the evidence was produced.

% ===== 2. Localisation =====================================================
\section{Your three changes, isolated (the ladder)}

Five arms on 50 facts, before touching the full pipeline: the original placement (attention V/O,
top third), the new placement (MLP up/down, middle third), each with and without paraphrase
diversity ($\geq$20 wordings incl.\ QA forms), and distillation from an in-context teacher into the
new placement. Trained-subset DRA at matched exposure, with background damage (BCP, lower is
better):

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Placement alone halves damage in all 8 of 8 comparisons} (e.g.\ Qwen BCP
      $3.6 \rightarrow 1.2$--$1.8$ at equal steps) and lifts recall off the floor.
\item \textbf{Paraphrases convert memorisation into extraction}: cloze accuracy falls while
      free-form recall rises --- the model stops reproducing a string and starts knowing a fact.
\item \textbf{Best arms}: Qwen-7B distil$\rightarrow$mid-MLP $0.36$ @ BCP $0.83$; Llama
      paraphrase-CE $0.353$. Against the old universal floor of $0.006$, the diagnosis was
      substrate and surface diversity --- exactly your reading.
\end{itemize}

% ===== 3. The third cause ==================================================
\section{The third cause: the safety machinery itself}

Integrating the winning recipe into the full autonomous pipeline initially \emph{erased} it:
with the safety machinery at its designed setting ($\delta_{\max}=0.01$ plus plasticity
down-scaling), trained-subset DRA is $0.007$ across three seeds --- indistinguishable from the old
floor. Relaxing clip and scaling in the identical pipeline yields $0.210 \pm 0.018$ at BCP
$0.90$--$0.99$, with 21--24 of $\sim$50 selected facts passing a direct greedy recall gate --- the
first externally verified autonomous consolidations in the project. The original failure therefore
had three stacked causes: wrong substrate, single-wording training, and safety machinery clipping
away the write. A moderate setting ($\delta_{\max}=0.1$, scaling on) preserves the recall
\emph{and} the protection; that setting is used everywhere below.

Every model sits at least $17\times$ above the old floor; the effect is strongest \emph{off} the
development model, which argues against overfitting the recipe to Qwen. Distillation beat plain
paraphrase-CE wherever both were tried (Mistral: $0.750$ @ $1.31$ vs $0.655$ @ $2.98$, seed 0) ---
the in-context-teacher write is the gentler write. The $1.5$B result suggests extraction capacity
grows with scale. Llama is the flagged exception: a pre-registered clip ladder
($\delta_{\max} \in \{0.1, 0.05, 0.03\}$) reduced damage monotonically ($4.35 \rightarrow 2.14$)
without reaching the $\leq 1.5$ bar --- its bleed is not clip-dominated, and we report the family
as \emph{not yet tuned} rather than excluded.

Three findings, each consistent across seeds:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The design-intent claim is now real}: SLEEP's cumulative recall exceeds naive's on
      all four models, on both seeds ($3\times$ on Qwen-7B). On Mistral it wins both axes
      simultaneously --- more recall \emph{and} less damage.
\item \textbf{The safety machinery is load-bearing at small scale}: naive fine-tuning detonates the
      $1.5$B model (BCP $24.5$ and $11.4$; the blow-up size is seed-random --- the plateau lottery
      of the 8~August report, reproduced), while SLEEP holds $\approx 2.4$ on both seeds.
\item \textbf{SLEEP's signature is stability}: its across-seed spread is $\approx 0.005$ on every
      model, versus naive's $0.04$--$0.06$. The earlier finding that fixed-horizon comparisons are
      seed-unreliable still stands --- for the \emph{unconstrained} arm. The constrained arm no
      longer exhibits the lottery.
\end{enumerate}

Llama repeats its single-cycle pattern (recall advantage, unacceptable compounding damage) and
keeps its flag.

% ===== 6. EWC ==============================================================
\section{The baseline that matters: EWC-only}

The 8~August report noted the uncomfortable finding that EWC alone looked competitive. The matrix
settles it with a matched-arms comparison (identical adapter, steps, and learning rate; the only
difference is the penalty):

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}l c c c@{}}
\toprule
Qwen2.5-7B, cycle 10 & SLEEP v2 & EWC-only & Naive \\
\midrule
Cumulative DRA & $\mathbf{0.173/0.175}$ & $0.060/0.052$ & $0.053/0.060$ \\
BCP            & $1.87/2.24$ & $1.53/1.80$ & $1.66/1.80$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The pre-registered prediction held exactly: EWC preserves (best damage numbers) but its recall sits
at naive's level. \textbf{Regularisation prevents forgetting; it does not create extraction.} SLEEP
delivers $3\times$ the recall of both baselines at comparable damage. On Llama, EWC-only is
competitive with SLEEP ($0.075/0.072$ at far better BCP) --- reported plainly; the Llama column is
not a SLEEP win.

% ===== 7. Paper state ======================================================
\section{The paper as it now stands}

The paper is being restructured from ``anatomy of a failure'' into the complete arc: diagnosis
(recognition--recall floor, proxy-validation failure, warm-up null --- all retained in full) $\to$
localisation of the cause (your three changes, isolated on the ladder) $\to$ the third cause (safety
machinery) $\to$ the validated system (the matrix above). The contribution set gains: a
causally-decomposed repair of the consolidation failure; cross-family validation at $n{=}5$ seeds;
the EWC dissociation (preservation $\neq$ extraction); and the small-model catastrophe that
right-sized machinery prevents. Named open gaps carried into future work: per-family learning-rate
tuning for Llama; a background-damage leak of $\approx +0.12$/cycle in all arms; and interleaved
rehearsal of prior consolidations (early batches still fade --- the mechanism biological sleep
actually uses).
